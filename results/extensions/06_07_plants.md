# 6-7. Factory downtime and productivity: what public sources support (Ram 1500 / Sterling Heights Assembly)

Plant-level production and headcount are not disclosed. This page logs the public events found, with a rough
output-per-worker proxy, so the tracker can be extended going forward. It is **descriptive only**.

| Date | Plant | Event | Source |
|---|---|---|---|

| Oct 2024 | SHAP | 191-199 jobs cut (reports differ; incl. 177 supplementals) ahead of Ram REV retooling | [Automotive News](https://www.autonews.com/manufacturing/stellantis-cutting-191-jobs-ram-1500-plant-michigan/), [CBS Detroit](https://www.cbsnews.com/amp/detroit/news/stellantis-laying-off-nearly-200-workers-sterling-heights-plant) |

**Output-per-worker proxy.** US Ram light-duty sales were 187k in 2024, 143k in 9M 2025 and 202k in 9M 2026. That
annualizes to about 270k in 2026, or about 45 trucks per SHAP worker at about 6,000 workers (Canada/Mexico volume
excluded). SHAP has no reliable 2024/2025 headcount, so no trend can be computed yet.

**What it says for the thesis.** The 2025 Hemi shortfall (orders above engine supply) and the 2026 seven-day schedules
point to supply-constrained, high-utilization Ram 1500 output in 2026. That fits the operating-leverage finding (test 52)
and the smaller Hemi discounts in item 1. It also means some of the 2026 volume jump is catch-up on 2025 Hemi orders,
not new run-rate demand.
