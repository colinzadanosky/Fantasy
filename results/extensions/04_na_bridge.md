## What is inside the 9M 2026 Industrial +1,334 (EUR bn)

| Component | 9M 2026 | Q3 2026 | Repeatable? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Regulatory (CAFE penalty elimination, OBBB Jul-2025) | +0.6 | +0.2 | Level persists while law stands; YoY gain ends once 2025 H2 is lapped (already only 0.2 in Q3). Policy, not efficiency. |
| Warranty: non-repeat of 2025 in-AOI change in estimate, net of higher warranty/recall | +0.0 to +0.5 | +0.0 to +0.3 | One-time base effect (the 2025 charge does not recur; nothing to repeat in 2027). |
| IEEPA tariff refund (Q1 2026) | +0.4 | +0.0 | One-time. |
| Underlying 2026 tariff cost vs 2025 | -0.4 | -0.1 to +0.1 | Recurring headwind (masked by refund). |
| Residual: manufacturing + purchasing efficiency incl. VCP, net of raw-material inflation, recall costs, lower D&A on impaired assets | +0.2 to +0.7 | -0.1 to +0.4 | Repeatable to the extent it is VCP/purchasing; part is lower depreciation after 2025 impairments (non-cash). |

**Answer.** Of the EUR 1.33bn, about **EUR 0.2-0.7bn (18%-55%)** is left for manufacturing/purchasing efficiency (VCP) after removing the items management itemises. About EUR 0.6bn (45%) is the CAFE penalty elimination: it keeps the cost level lower, but it is a policy windfall, not efficiency, and the YoY gain stops after mid-2026. EUR 0.4-0.9bn is one-time (the IEEPA refund and the warranty base effect), and the refund hides about EUR 0.4bn of recurring tariff cost.

Even the residual overstates cash efficiency. It is also net of raw-material inflation and higher recall costs (negative), and it includes lower depreciation after the EUR 5.7bn of NA platform impairments in 2025 (positive, non-cash).

**Restructuring is not in this bridge.** Stellantis excludes restructuring, impairments, the warranty re-estimate (EUR 3.25bn NA) and the CAFE write-offs from AOI. They sit in the reconciliation table, not in 'Industrial'.

**Disclosure limits.** Stellantis does not split 'Industrial' into purchasing, manufacturing, warranty and tariffs in the waterfall. The split above uses the group-level Cost of revenues commentary (warranty) and NA-specific text (regulatory, tariffs). The warranty range is therefore an upper bound for NA.
