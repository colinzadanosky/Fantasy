# 9. Used Ram 1500 resale: does the Hemi hold value? (MY2023, Dallas, 2026-11-11)

n = 54 used MY2023 Ram 1500 listings (Tradesman, Big Horn, Lone Star, Laramie), 27 Hemi and 27 V6.

| Spec | Hemi premium (log pts) | SE | p | n |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| trim + log mileage | +0.027 | 0.019 | 0.148 | 54 |
| trims sold with both engines only | +0.027 | 0.019 | 0.156 | 42 |

At the sample mean price ($32,335) the premium is about **$892**. Compare it with the Hemi option price on the original window sticker (not collected here). A used premium well below the option price means the Hemi's extra price did not survive into resale.

Read with care: this is a single-day sample from one metro, and the MY2023 'six-cylinder' is the 3.6 Pentastar V6, not today's 3.0 Hurricane. A clean test of the 2026 powertrain mix needs 2-3 years of MY2026 used listings.
